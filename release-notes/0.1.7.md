- **Closed/unwritable log fd tolerance during Fil-C panic logging (`runtime/filc/02-filc-panic-tolerate-closed-log-fd.patch`):** Updates `pas_fd_stream_vprintf()` in `libpas` to stop writing on non-`EINTR` `write(2)` errors (such as `EBADF` after a program closes `fd 2`, or `EPIPE` on a broken pipe) and zero-byte writes instead of tripping `PAS_ASSERT(errno == EINTR)` / `PAS_ASSERT(result)`, and drops the `PAS_ASSERT` around `write_panic_context()` in `filc_finish_panicking()`. Prevents a secondary/recursive panic (`__builtin_trap` / `SIGILL`) when a memory-safety violation happens after `stderr` is closed, allowing default panics to terminate cleanly via `pas_crash_with_info_impl` (`SIGTRAP`), `FILC_EXIT_ON_PANIC=1` to exit with status `42`, and `/var/filc/panics/` or `FILC_LOG_TO_FILE=1` to record the full readable safety panic report.
